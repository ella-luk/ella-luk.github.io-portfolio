Pocket Pantry

Pocket Pantry is an app that aims to make everything grocery related simple and accessible by providing a platform that assists with expiration tracking, recipe planning, and shopping list organization. Pocket Pantry was initially inspired by a fictitious app entitled "Uyu" from the book Rules for Rule Breaking by Talia Tucker that tracked the expiration dates for milk and provided dairy-based recipes for various items. I wanted to expand on this idea and design an app that included these original ideas but went beyond catering solely to dairy products.
